Planning an office move

  • Share a rough list of what is moving: desks, chairs, filing, shelving, equipment and boxes.
  • Tell us the floors and lift access at both buildings, and whether either building needs lift bookings or a loading bay.
  • Choose a date that suits your team, and ask about evenings and weekends where available.
  • Label desks and boxes by team or room.

What we can move

Desks and chairs, filing cabinets, shelving, boxes of stock and paperwork, and equipment. For computers and other IT equipment, back up your data and label cables before the move.

Keeping downtime low

A clear plan and a clear quote keep the day simple. With the Premium service our crew dismantles furniture where needed and carries everything out and in, so your team can keep working.
